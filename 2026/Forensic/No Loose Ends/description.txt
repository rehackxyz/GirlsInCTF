## No Loose Ends

**Category:** Forensics

**Difficulty:** Easy

**Flag Format:** `gctf26{...}`


A workstation inside the Finance department triggered a high-severity security alert shortly after suspicious activity was detected. By the time the incident response team arrived, the suspected payload had already been quarantined and removed from the system.

No weapon. No loot. No obvious evidence.

But every good heist leaves something behind.

Investigators managed to preserve network traffic from the affected environment. Reconstruct what happened, identify the quarantined artifact, and follow its digital fingerprint.

The file may be gone.

Its trail is not.

**Flag format:** `gctf26{Th3_H4sh_R3m3mb3r5}`
